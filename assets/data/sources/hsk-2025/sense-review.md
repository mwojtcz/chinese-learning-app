# Manual HSK sense review

On 2026-10-22, each of the 66 row-specific correction proposals from the
HSK 5–6 manual review was checked individually against its Chinese entry in
Wiktionary. This pass evaluated proposed corrections, not every word in either
HSK level; translation coverage is not evidence that a row has been reviewed.
The accepted changes are stored in `sense_overrides.json` and the corresponding
generated HSK 5–6 vocabulary files.

Forty-four HSK 5 proposals and all 21 HSK 6 proposals were accepted with edits
as needed. The HSK 5 proposal for [引进](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%BC%95%E9%80%B2)
was rejected: the entry supports both “recommend/endorse” and “introduce,” so
the existing meanings were retained. The rare Taoist sense “lead” for
[婴儿](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%AC%B0%E5%85%92) is valid; it is retained
with a usage label instead of being treated as a mistranslation.
